Verdict meanings:

- **CONFIRMED**: 2.1.101 introduced or activated the behavior, 2.1.108 retains it, and current TypeScript lacks the semantic behavior.
- **DUPLICATE**: the raw row is real, but it is only another manifestation of a confirmed canonical unit and should not be implemented independently.
- **REJECTED**: current TypeScript is behaviorally equivalent, the delta is presentation/performance-only without the claimed semantic gap, later architecture supersedes it, or HEAD deliberately chose a different non-101 behavior.
